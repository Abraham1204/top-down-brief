# House style

Organization-specific conventions. These override the general guidance in the other reference files. Edit this file to match your organization.

## Language and spelling
- Spelling: British English (organise, colour, programme, centre). If a draft consistently uses US spelling, keep the draft's convention and mention it once.
- Dates: 25 September 2026 in prose; Fri 2 Oct in deadlines (always include the weekday for deadlines).
- Times: 24-hour with time zone for external or cross-border readers (e.g. 14:00 GST).
- Currency: code before amount for non-local currencies (USD 40,000; AED 150,000); be consistent within a piece.
- Numbers: words for one to nine, numerals for 10 and above; always numerals for money, percentages and dates.

## Greetings and sign-offs
- External: "Dear [First name]," → "Kind regards," then full name, title, organization.
- Senior internal: "Dear [First name]," or "[First name]," → "Best regards,".
- Team / colleagues: "Hi [First name]," or "Hi all," → "Thanks," or "Best,".
- Formal letters: "Yours sincerely" (named recipient) / "Yours faithfully" (Dear Sir or Madam).
